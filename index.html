01Meet the Pitmaster

From a Sunday Pop-Up to a Saturday Sellout

Iron Jacket BBQ started small: owner Eric selling brisket and pulled pork out of a borrowed kitchen space, one day a week, until the line got too long to ignore. Today it's a proper storefront on Main Street in Mulvane — same oak-fired pit, same habit of selling out most Saturdays.

Everything is still smoked low and slow the Texas way, judged one bark-crusted slice at a time.
